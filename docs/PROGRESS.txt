## Toolchain
- Use the x64 **VS 2022 Build Tools** developer shell (MSVC 19.44, v143).
  - `& "C:\Program Files (x86)\Microsoft Visual Studio\2022\BuildTools\Common7\Tools\Launch-VsDevShell.ps1" -Arch amd64 -HostArch amd64`
- VS 2026 18.0 fails on this machine.
  - Its bundled vcpkg (2025-09-03) doesn't know the v145 toolset.
  - Its VS 2022 compiler folder is a broken leftover with no `cl.exe`.
  - To switch later: update VS 2026, remove that leftover in the VS Installer, and change the preset toolset hint `v143` to `v145`.
- vcpkg manifest mode with triplet `x64-windows-static-md`.
  - The registry baseline is pinned to `b1b19307`, the snapshot bundled with Visual Studio.
  - The build points vcpkg at the developer shell's VS instance (`VCPKG_VISUAL_STUDIO_PATH`).
- Keep the repo at a short path. Paths around 120 characters deep hit MAX_PATH (`C1083`).
- VS Code's CMake extension reconfigures automatically when `CMakeLists.txt` is saved. Don't configure from the command line at the same time.

## Commands (from `app/`)
- Configure: `cmake --preset windows-msvc`
- Build: `cmake --build --preset windows-msvc-release`
- Test: `ctest --preset windows-msvc-release`
- Run: `build\windows-msvc\release\psyight.exe`
