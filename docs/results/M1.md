### Diagnosis

`--detail` on the labels as they are:

- Detected onset minus labelled onset, for notes matched within 50 ms (all 452): median +17.7 ms, p5 +15.2, p95 +22.1.
- Detected kick minus the nearest grid beat (151 kicks): median +25.0 ms, p5 +24.6, p95 +25.3.

On the synthetic set the kick detector reads kicks 2.5 ms early, so the manual grid's first downbeat (and the labels snapped to it) is about 25 to 27.5 ms earlier than the track's kicks. The labels' error is a constant offset, not scatter: with the labels shifted to the kicks, every labelled note matches.

The +25 ms column scores a scratch copy of the labels with the grid, region and every note moved 25 ms later (the measured kick offset); the owner's file was not changed. To reproduce, shift `grid.first_downbeat_s`, the region and every `onset_s` and `offset_s` by +0.025 in a copy and pass it as the second argument.

Open question (SPEC A15): after the shift, detected onsets sit 7 ms before the kick-aligned 16ths (p5 to p95: 9.8 to 2.9 ms early). That is either the production's timing or tracker bias on real attacks; grid-snapped labels cannot tell them apart.

Against the SPEC 15 placeholder targets: onset F at least 0.90 on the synthetic set is met (1.000 on every case); gate-ratio MAE at most 0.10 is just met (0.100 averaged over the cases). Known v0 limits behind the larger gate errors:

- Gaps shorter than the 25 ms envelope window (ghost notes, gate 0.7 and above at 160 BPM) are not resolved: the note runs to the next onset.
- Long notes decay below k × peak (k = 0.25) before their gate closes.
- The 28 ms notes of "offbeat, gate 0.3" are about one period of F1, too short for a pitch; their wrong pitches carry low confidence.
