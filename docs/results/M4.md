M4's exit (SPEC 15): the student matches or beats DSP on the labelled regions, with measured inference latency. Only OculusDrift's minute (93.2 to 154.5 s, 452 notes) is labelled, so this is one region of one track: a first reading, not the exit table.

## Data

- 258 files, 180 tracks after removing 66 duplicates.
- 8 copies of the reference tracks were excluded: Hall of Purity and Hagios, three of them under other names. No copies of OculusDrift or Phase Change were in these folders.
- The teacher is the file-mode analysis of the Demucs stems, giving kick, hat and bass onsets, bass gate and pitch, and residual activity per 5.33 ms frame.
- The student sees the causal two-resolution spectrogram of the mix.
- Export took about 2.5 hours for 180 tracks on the laptop GPU and produced 3.4 GB of data. Stems were deleted per track.

## Model

A causal dilated-convolution network (two stacks of dilations 1 to 32): 359k parameters, 253 frames (1.35 s) of context, 2 frames (10.7 ms) of lookahead. Trained for 20,000 steps of 32 random 5.5 s crops, which took about 16 minutes on an RTX 2000 Ada laptop GPU. The best validation came at step 17,000.

Validation against the teacher on 18 held-out tracks (±4 frames, 21 ms):

| Head | Score |
|---|---|
| Kick onset F | 0.68 |
| Hat onset F | 0.79 |
| Bass onset F | 0.73 |
| Bass gate accuracy | 0.89 |
| Bass pitch accuracy | 0.67 |

## Labelled minute (OculusDrift, against hand labels, the same scoring as DSP)

| Metric | Student (causal, from the mix) | DSP (file mode, from the mix) |
|---|---|---|
| Notes labelled, detected, matched (±20 ms) | 452, 481, 451 | 452, 457, 452 |
| Onset precision, recall, F | 0.938, 0.998, 0.967 | 0.989, 1.000, 0.994 |
| Offset error abs median, abs p90 | 2.5 ms, 6.5 ms | 6.8 ms, 8.9 ms |
| Pitch accuracy, octave errors | 0.998, 0 | 0.989, 0 |
| Gate-ratio MAE | 0.106 | 0.037 |

The student beats DSP on offsets and pitch, matches it on recall, and trails it on precision (30 extra notes) and on gate ratio. The comparison is not like for like: DSP here runs in file mode and sees the whole track (non-causal), while the student is causal, as live mode needs. DSP has no causal mode yet to compare against.

Decoding used a 0.5 onset threshold. It was not tuned on this minute: tuning it here would leak the evaluation. A threshold chosen on the validation tracks is the next step.

## Decoder tuned on validation tracks

The first decoder had two faults, found by comparing its notes with the labels before changing anything:
- Half the extra notes were double detections: a second local peak of the onset curve 2.5 to 9 ms from a matched onset.
- 9% of matched notes were cut to one frame, because the causal gate lags the onset by a frame or two.

`m4_decode.py` now keeps only the strongest peak within 8 frames (43 ms; the DSP tracker's minimum gap is 45 ms), and lets a note end only after 4 frames (21 ms). `m4_tune.py` chose the thresholds on the 18 validation tracks against the teacher, never on the labelled minute.

| Setting | Onset F on validation (P, R) |
|---|---|
| threshold 0.5, no minimum gap (the first decoder) | 0.740 (0.682, 0.808) |
| threshold 0.5, 8-frame gap | 0.787 (0.780, 0.794) |
| threshold 0.4, 8-frame gap (chosen) | 0.790 (0.728, 0.864) |

For note length, the chosen gate threshold of 0.3 with a 4-frame minimum gives a mean absolute error of 2.68 frames, against 3.66 for gate 0.5 with no minimum.

Labelled minute, with the tuned decoder:

| Metric | Student, first decoder | Student, tuned decoder | DSP (file mode) |
|---|---|---|---|
| Notes detected, matched | 481, 451 | 465, 449 | 457, 452 |
| Onset precision, recall, F | 0.938, 0.998, 0.967 | 0.966, 0.993, 0.979 | 0.989, 1.000, 0.994 |
| Offset abs median, hit rate | 2.5 ms, 0.942 | 2.5 ms, 1.000 | 6.8 ms, - |
| Pitch accuracy | 0.998 | 1.000 | 0.989 |
| Gate-ratio MAE | 0.106 | 0.057 | 0.037 |

Then, after a second look at what remained:
- **Kick mask.** Extra 21 ms notes landed on kicks, where K-B-B-B bass doesn't start. Dropping bass onsets within 2 frames of the student's own kick peaks raised validation F by 0.004 to 0.010 at every threshold, so it was kept.
- **Sub-frame timing.** The remaining gate error clustered at one frame (5.33 ms, about 0.053 slot at 150 BPM). Onsets are now refined by a parabola and ends by linear interpolation of the gate crossing. The teacher is frame-quantised, so this was judged on the labelled minute only, not tuned there.

| Metric | Tuned | Tuned, kick mask and sub-frame timing | DSP (file mode) |
|---|---|---|---|
| Notes detected, matched | 465, 449 | 459, 447 | 457, 452 |
| Onset precision, recall, F | 0.966, 0.993, 0.979 | 0.974, 0.989, 0.981 | 0.989, 1.000, 0.994 |
| Gate-ratio MAE | 0.057 | 0.048 | 0.037 |
| Pitch accuracy | 1.000 | 1.000 | 0.989 |

About half of the 12 remaining extra notes fall between 142.7 and 144.0 s, where the labels have no notes. Someone needs to listen to that passage: it is either a fill the labels leave out, or real false detections.

Inference measured on an idle machine: 0.99 ms on the CPU (p99 1.39 ms) and 1.00 ms on DirectML (p99 1.09 ms).

## Inference latency

One call over the 253-frame receptive field (a live, once-per-hop call), with onnxruntime-directml 1.20.1, median of 300 calls:

| Provider | Median | p99 |
|---|---|---|
| CPU | 1.32 ms | 4.02 ms |
| DirectML (Intel graphics) | 1.43 ms | 1.61 ms |

One call per 5.33 ms hop is about 25% of one CPU core. Analysis latency is the 10.7 ms of lookahead, plus up to one hop of 5.33 ms, plus about 1.5 ms of inference: about 17 ms in total.

## In the app (2026-10-17)

The owner chose hand-written inference over ONNX Runtime (SPEC decision log). `tools/python/m4_export.py weights` folds batch norm into the convolutions and writes `data/m4/student.weights.{json,bin}`; `app/src/ml/student.*` loads it, runs the network and decodes notes as `m4_decode.py`.

Checks (`psyight_tests -tc="student*"`, on a committed fixture with random weights and inputs, `m4_export.py fixture`):
- Logits agree with onnxruntime within 2e-5.
- Streaming (one frame at a time), whole-track and any thread count give bit-identical logits.
- The decoder reproduces m4_decode.py's notes from the same logits.

On the labelled minute, `psyight_eval data\OculusDrift.mp3 --student` (features from the mix, the app's inference and decoder) gives exactly the Python path's numbers: 452 labelled, 459 detected, 447 matched, P 0.974, R 0.989, F 0.981, offsets |median| 2.5 ms, pitch 1.000, gate MAE 0.048. Features, inference and decoding of the whole 398 s track: 2.3 s.

Cost on this laptop's CPU, a random model of the student's size (`psyight_tests -tc="student: inference cost" --no-skip`): one streaming frame 37 µs median, p99 105 µs (onnxruntime: 1.3 ms per call over the receptive field); a 7-minute track (80,000 frames) 1.1 s. Live analysis latency is now the 10.7 ms of lookahead plus up to one 5.33 ms hop.

GUI: when the weights exist in the working directory's `data/m4/`, the student runs on load, and the bass roll's source choice (DSP or M4 student) sets the notes the roll and the Scoring tab show. Checked in the app on a scratch copy of OculusDrift: the Scoring tab shows the same counts as psyight_eval.
