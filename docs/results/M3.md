# M3 results (in progress)

M3's exit (SPEC 15) is a full reference track playing as a show on real strips at 60 fps or more, with the flash limiter verified. The strips and the F767 node are with the owner, so this page covers what can be measured without them: every frame of all four reference tracks rendered offline through the same show renderer the app uses (scene automation, limiters, patch and packets to the virtual node), and the show thread's timing.

Code: commit `2c1dd93` plus the tool, default project (the 444-pixel test frame, 10 A budget), default scenes. Commands, from the repository root after a Release build:

```
app\build\windows-msvc\release\psyight_show_check.exe --stems data\OculusDrift.mp3 "data\Phase Change.mp3" "data\Hall of Purity.wav" data\Hagios.mp3
app\build\windows-msvc\release\psyight_show_check.exe --stems --layers data\OculusDrift.mp3
app\build\windows-msvc\release\psyight_tests.exe -tc="show thread: timing" --no-skip
```

`psyight_show_check` exits with 3 if any second after the limiter holds more than the allowed flashes.

## Flash limiter over whole tracks (60 fps, Demucs-stem analysis)

A flash is a pair of opposing luminance changes of at least 0.1 (of full scale) over at least a quarter of the pixels; see `app/src/engine/limiters.h`. "Clipped" counts frames in which the limiter held rising pixels back.

| Track | Length | BPM | Flashes before, most in 1 s | Flashes after, most in 1 s | Frames clipped |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| OculusDrift | 398 s | 150.0 | 855, 5 | 784, 3 | 1400 (5.9%) |
| Phase Change | 437 s | 152.0 | 1207, 6 | 1024, 3 | 1626 (6.2%) |
| Hall of Purity | 452 s | 170.0 | 787, 8 | 605, 3 | 1279 (4.7%) |
| Hagios | 1042 s | 189.1 | 755, 9 | 663, 3 | 1557 (2.5%) |

No second of any track exceeds 3 flashes after the limiter.

Where the flashes come from (OculusDrift, each layer of each scene rendered alone over the whole track): the kick shockwave makes one flash per kick (747 kicks, 747 flashes, at most 3 in a second); the build compression 5, the drop burst 3, the staccato scene's plasma 6; the conveyor, shimmer, sparkle and the other plasmas none. On this 2.5 by 1.2 m frame the ring lights about 40% of the pixels as it crosses the long edges, so kicks alone use about 2.5 of the 3 flashes a second at 150 BPM, and the limiter holds back whatever else would flash in the same second. A narrower ring (0.12 m) was tried and is worse: it flashes twice per kick (the long edges, then both short sides together). How much of the flash budget kicks should take, and the ring's width, depend on the real rig and are the owner's call.

Two design faults this measurement found and fixed (commits `f1c0ec2`, `2c1dd93`):
- The conveyor stepped half its period per note at 50% duty, inverting the pattern on every 16th (a full-field strobe, against SPEC 7.2), and the shimmer lit every pixel in every rest. Before the fix the scenes made 3344 flashes on OculusDrift, up to 17 in a second.
- The limiter clipped each frame's rise to just under the threshold, which let a held strobe through as a 0.3-amplitude ramp, and judged frames before the power budget scaled them (Hagios reached 4 flashes in a second).

## Current, contrast and scenes

| Track | Current mean, p95, max | Power limited | Dark pixels mean (p10, p90) | Scene time |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| OculusDrift | 4.4, 8.3, 10.0 A | 0.1% | 32% (0, 76) | Groove 72%, staccato 4%, Drop 3%, Ambient 19%, Build 2% |
| Phase Change | 4.6, 8.8, 10.0 A | 0.2% | 37% (1, 77) | Groove 96%, Ambient 4% |
| Hall of Purity | 4.1, 8.0, 10.0 A | 0.3% | 29% (0, 77) | Groove 41%, staccato 8%, Drop 7%, Ambient 33%, Build 11% |
| Hagios | 4.4, 6.6, 10.0 A | 0.8% | 14% (0, 50) | Groove 2%, staccato 1%, Drop 19%, Ambient 67%, Build 12% |

Dark means luminance under 0.05. SPEC 7.7 suggests 30 to 50% dark most of the time [Likely]; the grooves meet it, the Ambient scene (plasma everywhere at 0.45) does not, which is why Hagios, two-thirds Ambient, sits at 14%. Current is the estimate at 20 mA per channel (SPEC 18 A5, [Verify] on the owner's strips).

## Timing

- Offline, render, limiters, patch and packets to the virtual node: p50 0.10 to 0.12 ms, p99 0.20 to 0.36 ms, max 0.56 to 1.20 ms a frame for 444 pixels.
- Show thread at 60 Hz (10 s): 600 frames, interval p5 15.6, p50 16.7, p95 17.8 ms, no late frames.
- In the app: 60.0 fps measured in the Output section; over a real socket to 127.0.0.1 a receiver got about 61 frames a second (universes 1 and 2 at 636 bytes, 3 at 438, sync at 49).
