# M2 results (in progress)

M2's exit table (SPEC 15: metrics for all four tracks, DSP against Demucs-assisted) needs a labelled region in each track. Only OculusDrift is labelled so far, so this page covers what can be measured now: the one labelled minute, and unlabelled sanity checks of the other three tracks.

Code: commit `c22a98f` plus later formatting, default parameters. Commands, from the repository root after a Release build:

```
app\build\windows-msvc\release\psyight_eval.exe data\OculusDrift.mp3 [--stems] [--detail]
app\build\windows-msvc\release\psyight_analyse.exe [--stems] [--kicks] [--tempo] data\OculusDrift.mp3 "data\Phase Change.mp3" "data\Hall of Purity.wav" data\Hagios.mp3
tools\python\.venv\Scripts\python tools\python\separate.py <audio...>
```

Stems come from Demucs v4 (htdemucs), about two minutes for all four tracks on an RTX 2000 Ada laptop GPU. They are aligned to the mix before analysis; OculusDrift's stems ran 47.0 ms ahead of the app's MP3 decoding.

Reading:

- **Tempo:** every minute of OculusDrift (150.0), Phase Change (152.0) and Hall of Purity (170.0) agrees with the whole-track tempo (`--tempo`). Hagios is about 187 BPM (owner) with long beatless passages at the start and end; its estimates are weak in both sources and disagree (187.65 against 189.13).
- **Downbeats:** right on OculusDrift (the only one with truth). Phase Change's mix and stems disagree by 3 beats; the owner's ear decides.
- **Bass:** the stems give more consistent pitch and higher confidence everywhere. Hall of Purity is in A# minor and very low (owner): A#0 is plausible, the spread over A0 to B0 is resolution at 29 Hz.
- **Kicks on Hall of Purity's drums stem (40%)** are fewer than on its mix (61%): unexplained, to check with labels.
- **Sections** (in the `psyight_analyse` report) look plausible on OculusDrift (intro to 16 s, a breakdown from 197 s with a build from 227 s and the drop at 240 s) and Phase Change, choppier on Hall of Purity, unusable on Hagios.
